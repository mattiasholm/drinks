# Southgate

## Ingredients
- 5 cl `rye whiskey`
- 1 cl `demerara syrup`
- 0.5 cl `Underberg`
- 1 `lemon peel`
- 8 `ice cubes`
- 1 `clear ice cube`

## Instructions
- Add clear ice cube
- Add rye whiskey
- Add demerara syrup
- Add Underberg
- Add ice cubes and stir for 30 seconds
- Strain
- Add lemon peel

## Glassware
- Old Fashioned glass

## Rating
- ★★★★★
